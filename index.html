About Us

A Global Force in Agricultural Trade

ARA Greenfield Limited is a major global merchant specializing in commodity exports, trading, and agricultural consulting with a strong global presence. Our expertise spans commodities including rice, corn, sesame, barley, sorghum, oats, cocoa, beans, wheat, soybeans, and precious metals — ensuring a reliable supply of high-quality products for both domestic and international buyers.

With an experienced leadership team, proprietary market intelligence tools, and a robust supplier network, ARA Greenfield Limited is committed to shaping the future of global commodity trade.
